Questions

Is Edge free?

Yes, every problem, game and test, with no ads.

Do I need an account?

No. Practice as a guest and your progress stays in your browser. An account syncs it across devices and puts you on the weekly leaderboard.

Where do the problems come from?

They're written for Edge: hand-written classics in our own words, plus generators that create many versions of well-known problem types with every answer computed exactly. None are copied from any firm.

Is Edge affiliated with a trading firm?

No. Edge is independent. Firm names only ever describe common interview formats.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. It works in any mobile browser and can be added to your home screen like an app.

What happens to my data?

No ads, no trackers, no cookies. You can download or delete your data at any time. Read the privacy policy.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

The short version

Edge has no ads, no tracking cookies and no cross-site trackers. We count visits with a privacy-friendly tool that doesn't use cookies. You can practice without an account. If you create one, we store only what's needed to run it: your email, your profile and your practice progress.

Without an account

Edge saves your progress in your browser's local storage under the key edge-v2. That includes solved problems, daily results, the days you practiced, ratings, XP and best scores. It stays on your device and isn't sent to us.

With an account

Accounts are run on Supabase, which hosts our login system and database. When you sign up we store:

  • your email address and a securely hashed password, handled by Supabase Auth;
  • your display name, plus the school, graduation year and target role if you choose to add them;
  • a copy of your practice progress, so it syncs across devices;
  • whether you want to appear on the leaderboard.

We use this only to run your account, sync your progress and show the leaderboard. We don't sell it or share it for advertising. If you opt in, your display name, XP, solved count and streak appear on the public leaderboard. Your email never does.

Signing in with Google

If you choose "Continue with Google", Google shares your name, email address and profile picture link with us so we can create your account. We use your name as your starting display name, which you can change. We don't get your Google password or access to anything else in your Google account.

Visitor statistics

We use GoatCounter to count visits, so we know which pages and games people use. It doesn't use cookies or track you across sites. For each page view it records the page, the referring site, your browser, screen size and approximate country. GoatCounter says it doesn't store IP addresses and only keeps a short-lived hash to count unique visits. We can't identify you from these statistics, and we never combine them with your account.

Problem reports and feedback

If you report a problem or send feedback, we store what you wrote, the type of report, the problem it's about, the page you sent it from and the time. If you're signed in, it's linked to your account. If you add an email for a reply, we store that too. We use reports only to fix problems and improve Edge. They aren't public.

If reporting isn't available yet when you send a report, it's kept in your browser and sent automatically later.

Weekly puzzle email

If you subscribe, we store your email address, when you subscribed and where you signed up. We use it only to send the weekly puzzle, never for anything else, and we never sell or share it. Every email includes a way to unsubscribe, or you can contact us to be removed.

Downloading and deleting your data

  • Download: Profile → Your data → Download my data saves everything Edge stores about your practice as a file.
  • Move: Import from a file merges a download into another browser.
  • Delete: Reset progress erases it from this browser. Delete account (on the Account page) removes your account and synced progress permanently.

Cookies and local storage

Edge sets no cookies. It uses local storage for your progress, your theme choice and, if you sign in, your login session. This storage is strictly necessary for features you ask for, so it doesn't require consent. We still tell you about it.

To remove it, clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Service providers

  • Supabase stores account data. See Supabase's privacy policy.
  • GoatCounter provides cookie-free visitor statistics. See GoatCounter's privacy policy.
  • Google handles sign-in if you choose "Continue with Google".
  • Google Fonts serves our typeface. Your browser's request reveals your IP address to Google, as described in Google's Privacy Policy.
  • jsDelivr serves the Supabase code library.
  • Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and request times.

Your choices

  • Edit your profile or leave the leaderboard at any time on the Account page.
  • Delete your account from the Account page. This permanently removes your profile and synced progress.
  • Contact us to ask what we hold about you.

Children

Edge is for people aged 13 and older. Don't create an account if you're under 13. If we learn that a child under 13 has an account, we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change how we handle data, we'll update this page and the date above before the change takes effect.

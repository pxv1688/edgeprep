7How many trailing zeros does 50! have?

FactorialsWarm-up

Show the answer and solution

Answer: 12

A trailing zero needs a factor of 10 = 2 × 5. Factors of 2 are plentiful, so count the 5s: multiples of 5 give one each, multiples of 25 an extra one, and so on. ⌊50/5⌋ = 10, ⌊50/25⌋ = 2. Total: 12.

9How many distinct arrangements are there of the letters in BANANA?

CountingWarm-up

Show the answer and solution

Answer: 60

Start with all 6! = 720 orderings, as if every letter were different. Swapping identical letters doesn't create a new word, so divide by 3! for the 3 As and 2! for the 2 Ns: 720 / 12 = 60.

10What is the sum of all integers from 1 to 50?

Answer: 1,275

Pair the first and last terms: 1 + 50 = 51, and every pair moving inward has the same sum. That's 50 terms averaging 25.5, so the sum is 50 × 25.5 = 1,275.

11What is the sum of the first 5 odd numbers?

29How many ways can you climb 5 stairs taking 1 or 2 steps at a time?

RecursionInterview

Show the answer and solution

Answer: 8

Look at the last move: it's either 1 stair (leaving 4 to climb before it) or 2 stairs (leaving 3). So f(n) = f(n − 1) + f(n − 2), with f(1) = 1 and f(2) = 2. Building up: 1, 2, 3, 5, 8. So f(5) = 8.

32How many ways can you climb 4 stairs taking 1, 2 or 3 steps at a time?

RecursionHard

Show the answer and solution

Answer: 7

Tribonacci recurrence: f(4) = 7.

  1. Look at the last move: it covers 1, 2 or 3 stairs. So f(n) = f(n−1) + f(n−2) + f(n−3).
  2. Base cases: f(0) = 1 (do nothing), f(1) = 1, f(2) = 2.
  3. Build up: f(3) = 4, f(4) = 7.
  4. So f(4) = 7.

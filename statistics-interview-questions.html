Statistics interview questions for quant roles

Quant research interviews lean on statistics: variance rules, standard errors, correlation, regression, the normal distribution and portfolio risk. Every answer below shows how to get there.

1For a constant a, Var(aX) equals…

  1. a · Var X
  2. a² · Var X
  3. Var X
  4. |a| · Var X

Warm-up

Show the answer and solution

Answer: a² · Var X

Var(aX) = a² Var X.

2The correlation between two random variables always lies between…

  1. 0 and 1
  2. −1 and 1
  3. −∞ and ∞
  4. 0 and ∞

Warm-up

Show the answer and solution

Answer: −1 and 1

|Corr| ≤ 1 by the Cauchy–Schwarz inequality.

3The central limit theorem says the mean of many iid draws with finite variance is approximately…

  1. uniform
  2. normal
  3. exponential
  4. Poisson

Warm-up

Show the answer and solution

Answer: normal

Standardized sample means converge to a normal distribution.

4A Poisson(λ) random variable has variance…

  4. 1/λ

Warm-up

Show the answer and solution

For a Poisson distribution, mean = variance = λ.

5Increasing a sample from 100 to 400 observations cuts the standard error of the mean by a factor of…

  4. √2

6Bayes' rule: P(A | B) equals…

  1. P(B | A) P(A) / P(B)
  2. P(A) P(B)
  3. P(B | A)
  4. P(A) / P(B)

Warm-up

Show the answer and solution

Answer: P(B | A) P(A) / P(B)

P(A|B)P(B) = P(B|A)P(A).

7A Type I error is…

  2. failing to reject a false null
  3. a coding bug
  4. a biased sample

Answer: rejecting a true null hypothesis

Type I is a false positive; Type II is a miss.

11For independent X and Y, Var(X + Y) equals…

  1. Var X + Var Y
  2. Var X − Var Y
  3. (Var X)(Var Y)
  4. 2 Cov(X,Y)

Warm-up

Show the answer and solution

Answer: Var X + Var Y

Var(X + Y) = Var X + Var Y + 2Cov, and Cov = 0.

12The standard error of a sample mean of n independent draws with SD σ is…

  2. σ/n

Answer: σ/√n

Var(mean) = σ²/n, so SE = σ/√n.

15An even-money bet wins 55% of the time. What fraction of your bankroll does the Kelly criterion say to bet?

16X and Y are independent with Var(X) = 4 and Var(Y) = 4. What is Var(1X + 1Y)?

VarianceWarm-up

Show the answer and solution

Answer: 8

Two rules: constants come out squared, and independent variances add with no covariance term. So 1² × 4 + 1² × 4 = 4 + 4 = 8.

17Draws have standard deviation 5. What is the standard error of the mean of 16 independent draws?

SamplingWarm-up

Show the answer and solution

Answer: 5/4

Averaging n independent draws divides the variance by n, so the spread shrinks by √n: 5/√16 = 5/4 = 1.25.

18Z is standard normal. What is P(Z > 0.5)? (Decimal.)

Normal distributionWarm-up

Show the answer and solution

Answer: 0.3085

Anchor on values worth memorizing: P(Z > 1) ≈ 0.159, P(Z > 1.645) ≈ 0.05, P(Z > 1.96) ≈ 0.025, P(Z > 2) ≈ 0.023, P(Z > 3) ≈ 0.0013. For z = 0.5: 1 − Φ(0.5) ≈ 0.3085.

19X ~ Binomial(10, 0.1). What is E[X]?

DistributionsWarm-up

Show the answer and solution

Answer: 1

X counts successes in 10 independent trials. Each trial contributes p = 0.1 on average, and expectations add: 10 × 0.1 = 1.

20X ~ Binomial(10, 0.1). What is Var(X)?

DistributionsWarm-up

Show the answer and solution

Answer: 9/10

Each trial is a 0/1 variable with variance p(1 − p) = 0.1 × 0.9 = 0.09. The trials are independent, so variances add: 10 × 0.09 = 0.9.

21Cov(X, Y) = 2, SD(X) = 2, SD(Y) = 4. What is Corr(X, Y)?

CorrelationWarm-up

Show the answer and solution

Answer: 1/4

Correlation is covariance with both spreads divided out: ρ = Cov/(SD(X) × SD(Y)) = 2/(2 × 4) = 0.25. It always lands between −1 and 1.

22X and Y are independent with Var(X) = 4 and Var(Y) = 4. What is Var(1X − 1Y)?

VarianceWarm-up

Show the answer and solution

Answer: 8

Two rules: constants come out squared, and independent variances add with no covariance term (so the minus sign disappears: (−1)² = 1). So 1² × 4 + 1² × 4 = 4 + 4 = 8.

29A portfolio holds 25% in asset A (vol 10%) and 75% in asset B (vol 15%), correlation 0.5. What is the portfolio volatility, as a decimal?

PortfoliosInterview

Show the answer and solution

Answer: 0.1269

Portfolio variance = w₁²σ₁² + w₂²σ₂² + 2w₁w₂ρσ₁σ₂ = 0.000625 + 0.01266 + 0.002813 = 0.01609. Take the square root for volatility: ≈ 0.1269. Because ρ < 1, that's below the weighted average of the two vols (0.1375): diversification.

30X is normal with mean 100 and SD 10. What is P(X > 70)? (Decimal.)

Normal distributionInterview

Show the answer and solution

Answer: 0.9987

Standardize: z = (70 − 100)/10 = -3. Below the mean, so P(X > 70) = 1 − P(Z > 3) ≈ 0.9987.

31A strategy averages 2 basis points a day with daily volatility 0.5%. What is its annualized Sharpe ratio (252 trading days, zero risk-free rate)?

PerformanceInterview

Show the answer and solution

Answer: 0.635

Daily Sharpe = mean ÷ volatility = 0.02% / 0.5% = 0.04. Over a year the mean grows with the number of days (×252) but volatility only with its square root (×√252), so the ratio grows by √252 ≈ 15.87: 0.04 × 15.87 ≈ 0.635.

32Cov(X, Y) = 2 and SD(X) = 2. What is the OLS slope when regressing Y on X?

RegressionInterview

Show the answer and solution

Answer: 1/2

The OLS slope is Cov(X, Y)/Var(X) = 2/2² = 2/4 = 0.5. On average Y moves that much for each one-unit move in X.

Mental math practice for trading interviews

Trading firms test arithmetic speed in their first-round online tests. Below are representative questions with the shortcuts that make them fast: splitting products, rounding to tens, building percentages from 10% and 5%, and scaling fractions.

1What is 12 × 57?

MultiplicationWarm-up

Show the answer and solution

Answer: 684

Split 57 into 50 + 7: 12 × 50 = 600 and 12 × 7 = 84. Add them: 600 + 84 = 684.

2What is 5% of 120?

PercentagesWarm-up

Show the answer and solution

Answer: 6

5% is half of 10%. 10% of 120 is 12, so 5% is 6.

4What is 14²?

SquaresWarm-up

Show the answer and solution

Answer: 196

Round to the nearest ten and correct: a² = (a − r)(a + r) + r². Here 14² = 10 × 18 + 16 = 180 + 16 = 196.

7Write 11/16 as a decimal.

Show the answer and solution

Answer: 11/16 ≈ 0.6875

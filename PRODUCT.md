# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated, with the user's requirement that it be "a real website with privacy policies, cookies etc." Chosen: a multi-page static site (plain HTML/CSS/JS, no build step) so it can be hosted free on GitHub Pages or Vercel. Pages: the app (index.html), a privacy policy, terms of use, and a storage/cookie notice. No backend yet; accounts and global leaderboards are future work.

## Users

College students recruiting for quant trader, quant researcher, and quant developer internships and new-grad roles. They practice in short sessions between classes and in the weeks before online assessments and superdays.

## Product Purpose

Edge is a quant interview trainer built as games. It turns each interview skill (mental math, probability, market making, bet sizing, options, coding, statistics) into a timed, scored game with explanations, so practice becomes a daily habit and users walk into interviews ready. Success: users come back daily and their skill ratings rise.

## Positioning

Question banks give lists; Edge gives games. Every topic is playable and scored, practice mirrors the formats trading firms actually use (80-in-8 style arithmetic, make-a-market rounds, betting games), a daily puzzle with streaks and shareable results builds the habit, and every answer teaches the method, not just the number.

## Operating Context

Used on laptops and phones, in short sessions. Progress (streak, XP, per-skill ratings, best scores, daily results) is stored only in the visitor's browser via localStorage. No accounts, no server, no analytics.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Modes: Daily Puzzle, Speed Math, Probability, Market Making, Betting Table (Kelly), Desk Quiz (options / code / stats).
- Question content must be original wording. Classic, widely known problems may be used in Edge's own words; never copy text from paid books or proprietary firm questions.
- No invented claims: no fake user counts, testimonials, firm endorsements, or placement rates.
- Firm names may be used only descriptively (e.g. "formats used by trading firms"), never as endorsement or branding.
- Legal pages are templates written by Claude, not legal advice; owner contact details are open decisions.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Edge. The user wants it to feel premium, not AI-generated, and to retain users.

## Evidence on Hand

None. No users, testimonials, or results exist yet; do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles

1. Play first: every screen should get the user into a rep within one click.
2. Teach the method: every answer explains why.
3. Honest difficulty: real interview standard, no inflated scores.
4. Habit over binge: daily puzzle, streaks, and visible skill growth.
5. Private by default: nothing leaves the browser.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-operable games, visible focus, reduced-motion support, readable on phones.
